01 · The idea

The D is a needle carrying its thread.

The stem is a sewing needle and the bowl is the thread that runs through its eye, loops round, and tucks back behind it. The loose end of the thread hangs to the left like draped fabric. Thick at the curve and fine at the ends, the thread has the same contrast as a classic couture serif.

02 · Sketches

Eight Ds from the sewing box

A button, a tape measure, a running stitch, pleats, a spool, a fold, a pin. Most were too literal or broke down at small sizes. Needle + thread was the one that still read as a D at 16 pixels.

03 · Geometry

Built on two ellipses

The outer bowl is a 70-unit curve and the inner one is 40 units wide, so the thread swells to its full weight on the right and thins to a hairline at the top and bottom. The needle tip drops below the baseline, the way a descender does.

04 · Logo suite

Mark, wordmark, lockups

The wordmark is set in Fraunces Light. The accent on the ó is redrawn as a tiny needle, so the name carries the mark inside it. Use the mark alone where space is tight: the profile picture, a woven label, a button.

05 · Signature details

Small things that repeat

Viral Toolkit reuses its play button everywhere. Dória does the same with three sewing details, used one at a time.

The needle accent

Taken from the ó. Use it as a bullet, a cursor, or a sticker on stories, always tilted 32°.

The running stitch

A dashed line of 14-unit stitches and 9-unit gaps. It replaces plain rules and dividers.

The needle pattern

For tissue paper, garment bags and story backgrounds. Keep it at 10–15% opacity.
