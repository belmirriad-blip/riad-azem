Story

Named after the wind that leaves the Sahara.

Ostro is the warm south wind that carries Sahara dust north over the sea. OSTRO starts where that wind starts: in Béchar, at the edge of the desert.

We make heavyweight, boxy menswear. The first piece is one cut-and-sew hoodie with curved contrast side panels, sewn to order by the founder's mother and delivered across Algeria, cash on delivery.

Mark · The idea

Two pen strokes, shaped like a dune.

A dune has two sides: the windward side, long and curved, and the slip face, short and steep. The O is drawn the same way with a flat pen held at 38°. The left stroke is one continuous curve. The right stroke is cut into flat facets, the way blackletter is. Nothing runs through the counter and nothing sticks out.

Mark · Sketches

Eight Os on the way to the dune

Every version was drawn with the same pen and the same angle, then tested at 32 and 20 pixels. A plain textura O looked like any gothic font. The round one lost the pen. The open head, the lozenge and the inner spur stopped reading as an O. The curved-and-cut version kept its counter open at small sizes and looks like nothing else.

Mark · Geometry

One pen, one angle, one unit

The pen is 19 units wide on a 100-unit cap height, held at 38°. That angle makes the strokes thin where they meet at the head and the foot and heavy down the sides. The height of the counter is the unit x: it sets the clear space around every lockup.

  • Cap height100
  • Width72
  • Pen19 at 38°
  • Counter x76

Lockups

The mark leads, the name follows

The wordmark is OSTRO in Archivo ExtraBold, always in capitals, tracked at 0.35em. In the primary lockup the mark sits centred above it. The dashed line is the clear space: one counter height, x, on every side. Nothing else goes inside it.
