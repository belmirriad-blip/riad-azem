Story

Named after the wind that leaves the Sahara.

Ostro is the warm south wind that carries Sahara dust north over the sea. OSTRO starts where that wind starts: in Béchar, at the edge of the desert.

We make heavyweight, boxy menswear. The first piece is one cut-and-sew hoodie with curved contrast side panels, sewn to order by the founder's mother and delivered across Algeria, cash on delivery.

Mark · The idea

Two dunes with the wind between them.

In the Sahara, a wind that always blows from one direction builds crescent-shaped dunes called barchans. The OSTRO mark is two of them, turned to face each other inside a heavy circle. The space between them is the path of the wind: thin where it enters and leaves the circle, open in the middle, where it becomes the counter of the O. Solid, calm, and readable as a letter at any size.

Mark · Sketches

Eight Os on the way to the dunes

Every version starts from the same heavy circle and only changes the counter. They were all tested at 32 and 20 pixels. The plain lens was clean but common. The crescent, the off-centre hole and the oval stopped reading as an O. The first split version had steps where the gap met the counter. Drawing the gap and the counter as one pair of curves fixed it, and kept a clear O.

Mark · Geometry

One circle, two curves

The mark is a circle 100 units across. Two much larger circles, radius 95, overlap through its middle. Where they overlap, the circle is cut away: 32 units wide at the centre, narrowing to 6 units where it leaves the edge. The two big circles are staggered by 6 units, so the cut leans like a gust, and the whole cut is turned 25° to the left. The widest part of the counter is the unit x: it sets the clear space around every lockup.

Lockups

The mark leads, the name follows

The wordmark is OSTRO in Archivo ExtraBold, always in capitals, tracked at 0.35em. In the primary lockup the mark sits centred above it. The dashed line is the clear space: the width of the counter at its widest, x, on every side. Nothing else goes inside it.
