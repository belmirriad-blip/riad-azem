عزم: resolve you act on. Azm teaches Algerian Bac students how to study: a plan, past exam exercises, a daily log, and follow-up. It is not tutoring.

01 · The message

Students don't fail the Bac because it's hard. Nobody taught them how to study.

Who it's for

3AS students, mostly Sciences expérimentales, aged 16 to 19. Many average under 10/20. And the parents who pay and worry.

What it teaches

The method before the hours: a weekly plan, past exam exercises, logging every session, and follow-up.

Who's behind it

Riad, a medical student who went from 10 to 16 on the Bac in about three months by studying this way.

How it sounds

Calm, serious, honest, practical. A steady older brother who did it, not a motivational coach.

02 · The mark

An ع whose curve keeps climbing

The mark is the first letter of عزم, drawn as one even stroke with rounded ends. The lower curve doesn't close. It drops, turns, and climbs back up past where it started, like steady progress. One step at a time, not a rocket. It's a single colour with no fine detail, so it still reads at 16 pixels.

Three variations

Pick one and everything gets rebuilt from it. Variation 1 is in use until you choose.

06 · Voice

Concrete actions, real numbers, no hype

Simple Modern Standard Arabic in the app, Darija is fine on social, French for the terms students already use. Never promise a grade. Address each student in the form that matches their gender.

07 · Social

Templates with a lot of empty space

At most two colours plus white, no photos, no emoji. One idea per post. The testimonial card is a blank template: fill it with a real student's grades and words, first name or initials only.
