01 · The idea

The D is a blade with a fang.

There is no straight stem. One curved blade wraps the whole letter: thick on the right, sharpened to a gothic point at the belly, and thinning into two claws on the left. The stem is a fang hanging from the top bar. It stops short of the bottom, so nothing runs through the letter and nothing crosses.

02 · Sketches

Eight Ds on the way to the fang

Two crescents, split blades, wings, a leaf, a leaf pushed through the bars, a pointed belly. The early ones read as “))” or as an eye, and the pierced leaf put a line through the letter. Hanging the blade from the top bar as a fang was the one that read as a D at 20 pixels.

03 · Geometry

One blade, one fang

The bowl runs from the cap line to the baseline with no overshoot, so it sits level with the capitals next to it. The belly comes to a point on the right instead of a round curve. The fang's tip stops on its own line, well clear of the bottom bar.

04 · Logo suite

The mark lives inside the name

The logo sits above the name, and the first D of DORIAN is the logo itself, set at the height of the capitals in Archivo ExtraBold. Inside the name the D is cut slightly heavier so it matches the weight of the letters next to it. Use the D alone where space is tight: the profile picture, a woven label, a button.

05 · Signature details

Small things that repeat

Viral Toolkit reuses its play button everywhere. DORIAN does the same with three details taken from the mark, used one at a time.

The fang

Lifted from the D. Use it as a bullet, a cursor, or a sticker on stories, tilted 12°.

The running stitch

A dashed line of 14-unit stitches and 9-unit gaps. It replaces plain rules and dividers.

The fang pattern

For tissue paper, garment bags and story backgrounds. Keep it at 10–15% opacity.
